Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

ToolTomato ("we", "us", "our") operates tooltomato.in (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information we collect

Most tools on ToolTomato run entirely in your browser — numbers you type into a calculator are not sent to our servers. We may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, such as:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Approximate location (city/country) derived from IP address
  • Pages visited, time spent, and referring site
  • Cookies and similar identifiers (see Section 3)

We do not require account creation, and we do not knowingly collect names, phone numbers, or payment details through the tools themselves.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its tools
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns (analytics)
  • To serve advertising, including personalised ads where permitted (see Section 4)
  • To respond to messages sent through the Contact page

3. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to remember your cookie preference, understand how the Site is used, and support advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; blocking cookies may affect how some parts of the Site work.

4. Advertising & third parties

We may display ads through third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. We may also use analytics providers such as Google Analytics to understand Site traffic; these providers have their own privacy policies governing the data they process.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited technical data with service providers (hosting, analytics, advertising) strictly to operate the Site, and may disclose information if required by law.

6. Children's privacy

ToolTomato is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information we process, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may clear cookies at any time through your browser, use browser "Do Not Track" settings, or opt out of personalised ads using the links in Section 4. You may also contact us to ask what data, if any, we hold about you.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page or to support@tooltomato.in.
