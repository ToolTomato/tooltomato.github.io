Why we started ToolTomato

Most calculators and everyday tools online are buried under pop-ups, forced sign-ups, or paywalls for things that should take ten seconds. ToolTomato exists to fix that: one place for the small, useful tools people reach for every day — EMI, GST, age, percentages, unit conversions — plus a few AI-powered tools for writing and images.

What we believe

  • No sign-up, ever. Every tool works the moment you land on the page.
  • Fast over flashy. Tools should load quickly and get to the point.
  • Honest about limits. Our calculators give general guidance, not professional advice — see our Disclaimer.
  • Ad-supported, not data-hungry. We keep the site free using ads, not by selling your information. Details in our Privacy Policy.

Who's behind it

ToolTomato is an independently run project focused on building simple, dependable web tools for Indian users. We're a small team (and growing), and we read every message that comes in through the Contact page.
